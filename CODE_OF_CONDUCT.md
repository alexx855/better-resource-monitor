# Code of Conduct

Better Resource Monitor is a small open-source project. Keep contributions practical, respectful, and focused on improving the app.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, kind, and technical.
- Assume good intent when a report is incomplete.
- Keep bug reports and pull requests focused.
- Respect user privacy and avoid requesting unnecessary personal data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Spam, trolling, or repeated off-topic comments.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Abusive behavior toward contributors or users.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove disruptive content and may block accounts that repeatedly violate this code of conduct.

For concerns, email `hello@alexpedersen.dev`.
